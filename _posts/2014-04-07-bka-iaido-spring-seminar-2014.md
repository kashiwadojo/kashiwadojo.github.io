Just back from a great iaido spring seminar in Stevenage (05/04/14-06/04/14). Very intensive training and lots of detailed technical information relayed to us from the senior Japanese delegation who attended the High-Grade Seminar a few weeks ago. One of the best organised seminars I've been to, with a great balance of training and discussion (i.e. not too much discussion!). Many thanks to the BKA iaido bu for all their efforts.

Congratulations go to Kashiwa student Rafal Radosz (pictured), who passed his iaido Ikkyu grading on the Sunday.
