On Sunday the 7th September Kashiwa Dojo was delighted to host the Great Britain Jodo Squad for their last intensive training session before the European Jodo Championships on the 13th/14th September in Turin, Italy. Kashiwa Dojo jodo teacher Will Heal, selected for the squad again this year for the team event, had a great day training with the some of the best jodoka in the UK under the excellent supervision of Harry Jones and Andy Watson, two of the most sought-after jodo teachers in Europe.
