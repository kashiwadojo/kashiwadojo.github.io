Welcome to Kashiwa Dojo!

Kashiwa Dojo is a friendly and inclusive dojo in South London (UK), dedicated to the study of the traditional Japanese martial arts of iaido and jodo. We are affiliated to the Britisk Kendo Association (BKA), the European Kendo Federation (EKF) an the Internatinal Kendo Federation (IKF). All coaches are fully qualified and insured through the BKA.
